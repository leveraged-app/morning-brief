Stocks1 of 3

AI and chip stocks trigger worst S&P 500 day since April 2025

Why it matters

A handful of AI-linked megacaps now carry so much index weight that trouble in them hits the whole market. Roughly $2.5 trillion of market value was erased in one session.

Why it happened

Investors dumped chip and data-center names on fears that Democratic gains in the midterms could mean tougher AI regulation and slower data-center construction. Nvidia fell more than 9%, and the Nasdaq closed in correction territory. Chip stocks stabilized and the Nasdaq rebounded on Friday.

Macro2 of 3

Hot US jobs report keeps Fed rate-hike fears alive

Why it matters

Strong data plus higher oil-driven inflation makes it harder to argue for easier policy. Tuesday's CPI is the next test, with annual inflation expected near 3.9%.

Why it happened

US employers added 165,000 jobs in October against about 59,000 expected, and unemployment slipped to 4.1%. Markets are pricing another 25 bp Fed hike in December.

Canada3 of 3

Canada's job losses deepen as the loonie weakens

Why it matters

A softening labour market while the Fed leans hawkish leaves the Bank of Canada in a tougher spot and keeps the loonie under pressure.

Why it happened

Statistics Canada reported 4,700 jobs lost in October, against an expected gain, and the unemployment rate rose to 6.7%. That is a third straight monthly decline, and the US-Canada gap in labour strength weighed on the Canadian dollar.
