Headline

AI chip rout sends volatility soaring

Mood

Risk-off, led by tech. AI and tech stocks plunged on worries that the election could slow AI spending. The Nasdaq then bounced, though investors stayed cautious.

What moved and why

AI and chip stocks trigger the worst selloff in over a year [Stocks]
Why it happened: Investors pulled back from tech on concerns about data center spending after the midterm elections. The PHLX Semiconductor Index fell 12.2%.
Why it matters: The AI buildout is closely tied to the wider economy, so the damage spread beyond tech. All 11 S&P 500 sectors ended lower.

Strong US jobs data revives Fed rate-hike talk [Rates]
Why it happened: The US added 165,000 jobs, far above consensus, and Treasury yields and rate-hike odds rose. Earlier, the flight from tech had pushed yields lower.
Why it matters: Another 25 bp hike is priced in for December. That makes the coming inflation data more important.

Canada's job market weakens [Canada]
Why it happened: Employment fell 4,700, after losses of 68,000 in September and 42,000 in August. The unemployment rate hit a six-month high of 6.7%. The soft data may have added to the Canadian dollar's weakness against the US dollar. I didn't find a source that confirms this.
Why it matters: Traders read this report for signs of slack that could shape Bank of Canada policy.

Deals

  • Marvell to acquire Lumentum. All-stock M&A, with an implied enterprise value of $115 billion. The logic: it joins Marvell's chip and interconnect strengths with Lumentum's lasers and optical products. Closing is expected in the first half of 2028, pending approvals.
  • Blackstone-led group buys LNG Canada stake. Buyers: a Blackstone-led group including CPP Investments, OMERS, IMCO and PSP. Target: Mitsubishi's 49.9% stake in LNG Canada's business. Size: $3.1B. Logic: large pension funds and infrastructure investors want long-life Canadian energy export assets.
  • National Bank of Canada to acquire Embark Student Corp. Type: bank M&A, announced Nov. 6. Size: not found in the sources. Embark manages RESPs for about 436,000 beneficiaries and has over $6 billion in assets. The logic: it adds a digital education-savings platform to the bank's retail business.

What's coming today

  • Saturday: US and Canadian markets are closed, and I found no major releases scheduled.
  • Monday, 10:00 ET: Chicago Fed's Goolsbee speaks, one of several Fed speakers this week. This is the next read on rate-hike appetite.
  • Monday, 1:00 PM ET: a $58B US 3-year note auction, a gauge of demand for Treasuries.
  • Monday earnings: Constellation Energy, Barrick, Occidental and Rocket Lab.
  • Tuesday, 8:30 ET: US October CPI, the key input for the December Fed call.

Canada corner

  • Alberta led provincial job losses, down 24,000. Wholesale and retail trade lost 29,000 jobs.
  • Canadian Natural is targeting $800 million a month in share buybacks in November and December.
  • Montreal-based Avenir Global agreed to buy marketing firms from UK-listed Next 15.
  • Several Canadian companies report next week. These include CAE, CGI, Franco-Nevada and Power Corp.

The takeaway

If someone asks you about markets today: an AI-spending scare drove a sharp tech selloff and a jump in volatility. Strong US jobs data and weak Canadian jobs data keep rates and the Fed in focus, and Tuesday's US CPI is the next big test.
