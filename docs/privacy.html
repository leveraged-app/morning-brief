Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

The short version: We collect only what the app needs: your email to sign you in, plus the profile and progress you choose to add. Some of it shows on leaderboards. We don't sell your data or show ads. You can delete your account at any time in Settings.

1. Who we are

Leveraged is a student-run finance learning app based in Ontario, Canada. Contact us at leveragedbrief@gmail.com about anything in this policy.

2. What we collect

  • If you only read or listen: no account is needed. Your lesson progress, level, track and settings are saved in your own browser on your device.
  • If you create an account: your email address, then the display name, username, school, track and level you enter, plus your XP, streak, lesson progress, follows, club memberships and market calls.
  • Technical information: our hosting and font providers receive standard information such as your IP address and browser type when pages load.

3. Why we use it

  • To sign you in with an emailed link and keep your account secure.
  • To save and sync your progress across devices.
  • To run leaderboards, friends, clubs and market calls.
  • To keep the app working and prevent abuse.

We don't sell your personal information, use it for advertising, or send marketing emails. If we ever add a newsletter, it will be opt-in with an unsubscribe link.

4. What others can see

Other users can see your display name, username, school, XP, streak and club memberships on leaderboards, in search and in clubs. Your email address is never shown to other users.

5. Who we share it with

We use these service providers to run the app. They handle data on our behalf:

  • Supabase: accounts and database (servers may be in the United States)
  • Google (Gmail): sends sign-in emails
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr: deliver fonts and code files

Your information may be stored and processed outside Canada, including in the United States, where it may be subject to that country's laws. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account information until you delete your account. When you delete it, your profile, progress, XP, follows, club memberships and market calls are permanently removed. Backups held by our providers may take a short time to clear.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: Settings → Account → Delete account.
  • Change your details: edit your profile in the Network tab.
  • Access or correct your data, or ask a question: email us and we'll respond within 30 days.
  • If you're not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

8. Age

Accounts are for people 16 and older. If you believe someone younger has made an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

9. Security

We use sign-in links instead of passwords, encrypted connections and database rules that limit who can read and change data. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep access to your email safe.

10. Changes

We'll update this page if anything changes and note significant changes in the app.
